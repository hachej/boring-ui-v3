# Actor invariants

## ACTOR-1 — attributable identity

A human, Agent or service has a stable attributable identity. Provenance-sensitive Agent work binds the exact definition/instruction/skill revisions used; changing configuration does not rewrite earlier work or change Actor identity by accident.

## ACTOR-2 — Actor memory is not business truth

Model output, memory, transcript and session state may propose results but do not become Job, Resource or authority state. Deleting a session cannot remove the sole record of an accepted result; that record belongs to Job/Resource authorities.

## ACTOR-3 — Actors do not manufacture authority

Declared tools, files and capabilities are requests, not grants. Actors receive issued operations; model arguments cannot replace trusted Job/Actor/scope context, widen access or reach provider/policy administration. Authoritative mutation requires an admitted operation.

## ACTOR-4 — credentials stay outside definitions

Provider/API credentials belong to trusted Environment/provider services, never Agent definition Resources or model-facing configuration.
